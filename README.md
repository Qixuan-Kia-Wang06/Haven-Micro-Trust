# 🏦 Haven-Micro-Trust — An Inclusive Online Trust Platform for the Silver Economy

> 🚀 ECON1501-*Introduction to FinTech* Course Project | An intelligent trust management app powered by Google AI Studio

## 📖 Background

As population aging accelerates, the "Silver Economy" is becoming a crucial component of socioeconomic development. In the realm of wealth inheritance and retirement planning, **Family Trusts**, as a mature financial instrument, should play a more significant role. However, the current market presents distinct pain points:

1. **High Barriers to Entry (Lack of Inclusivity):** Traditional trusts are almost exclusively designed for High-Net-Worth Individuals (HNWI), excluding middle-class families who also have strong needs for wealth planning and risk isolation.
2. **Exorbitant Communication & Time Costs:** Establishing a traditional trust relies heavily on tedious offline communication, due diligence, and manual document drafting, resulting in high labor costs.
3. **Outdated Processes & Poor Digital Experience:** The industry lacks modern digital portals, leading to opaque information and a suboptimal customer experience.

To address this, we designed and developed **SHaven-Micro-Trust**, an **inclusive online trust App** targeting the underserved mass market and middle-class families, aiming to make trust services accessible to every household.

## ✨ Core Concept & Solutions

This project aims to break down the barriers of traditional trusts by deeply integrating finance with technology, thereby reshaping the trust service workflow:

* **🌐 End-to-End Digitalization:** Transitioning traditional offline processes—such as due diligence, term negotiation, and contract signing—entirely online to significantly shorten the business cycle.
* **🤖 AI-Empowered Cost Reduction:** Integrating **Google AI Studio** to provide underlying LLM capabilities. The AI assistant conducts preliminary needs assessments and automatically generates basic trust frameworks, drastically reducing human advisory costs.
* **👵 Silver-Friendly Design:** Designed not only for the children of the middle class but also tailored for the elderly themselves, featuring a minimalist UI, accessible interactions, and clear, visual asset dashboards.
